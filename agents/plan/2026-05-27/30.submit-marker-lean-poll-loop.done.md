## Goal
Reduce Experiment 2 hot-loop measurement overhead so the offset-1 cumulative trace is closer to the actual submit-to-first-completion observation path.

## Scope

- Keep submit timing and cumulative marker-relative timestamps.
- Rerun offset-1 hardware data and compare cumulative first-visibility timing.
