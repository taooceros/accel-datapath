# Submit-marker traced latency results, 2026-05-27

## Question

Experiment 1 showed the one-extra-submit latency bends around 115--117 outstanding requests. The rewritten experiment 2 asks a narrower question:

> Before or at that submit-admission wall, are early descriptors already completing, and what does the observation poll cost?

## Setup

- Binary: `./target/release/hw-eval`
- Device: `/dev/dsa/wq0.0`
- WQ mode: dedicated
- CPU pin: core 0
- Operation: DSA `noop`
- Burst length: `N=160`
- Marker position: first descriptor
- Poll step: fixed 1 after the configured offset
- Tracked completions: first four completion records starting at the marker
- Artifacts:
  - `docs/report/benchmarking/submission_bottleneck_2026-05-27/marker_trace_offsets_96_112_115_noop.raw`
  - `docs/report/benchmarking/submission_bottleneck_2026-05-27/marker_trace_offsets_96_112_115_noop.json`
  - `docs/report/benchmarking/submission_bottleneck_2026-05-27/summary_marker_trace_offsets.csv`

## Command

```bash
cargo build --release -p hw-eval

launch ./target/release/hw-eval \
  --accel dsa \
  --benchmark submit-marker-overlap \
  --device /dev/dsa/wq0.0 \
  --json \
  --iterations 1000 \
  --marker-bursts 160 \
  --marker-positions first \
  --marker-poll-offsets 96,112,115 \
  --dsa-op noop \
  --pin-core 0
```

## Result summary

All rows completed every submitted descriptor: median completed `160`, median missing `0`, median errors `0`.

| poll offset | first traced submit | comp1 first median-success submit | comp2--4 first median-success submit | first trace visible prefix median | notable submit median |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 96 | 96 | 97 | 96 | 0 | submit #97 median 472 TSC ticks after the first poll |
| 112 | 112 | 112 | 112 | 4 | submit #112 median 30 TSC ticks; later traced submits median 28 TSC ticks |
| 115 | 115 | 115 | 115 | 4 | submit #116 median 470 TSC ticks immediately after first poll at the wall |

Selected trace points:

| offset | submit index | submit TSC median | visible prefix median | visible count median | comp1 success fraction | comp1 success-poll TSC median |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 96 | 96 | 30 | 0 | 3 | 0.016 | 78 |
| 96 | 112 | 28 | 4 | 4 | 1.000 | 24 |
| 112 | 112 | 30 | 4 | 4 | 1.000 | 72 |
| 112 | 115 | 28 | 4 | 4 | 1.000 | 22 |
| 115 | 115 | 28 | 4 | 4 | 1.000 | 70 |
| 115 | 116 | 470 | 4 | 4 | 1.000 | 22 |

## Interpretation

The first four NOOP completions were already visible at the first poll when tracing started at submit 112 or submit 115. That means early descriptors had reached DSA and completed by the admission-wall region; the cheap-submit prefix was not merely hiding work that only became device-visible after the wall.

The offset-96 row shows completion visibility order is not a strict prefix: at the first poll, completions 2--4 were visible in the median sample while completion 1 was not. This matches the DSA ordering rule that completion records need not become visible in descriptor order.

The first success poll was more expensive than later hot-success polls: comp1 success-poll median was 72 TSC ticks at the first offset-112 poll and 70 TSC ticks at the first offset-115 poll, then about 22 TSC ticks once the line was hot. This is observation cost, not submit cost. The submit measurement itself stayed around 28--30 TSC ticks at the first traced points, except the offset-115 run observed a 470 TSC tick submit at index 116.
