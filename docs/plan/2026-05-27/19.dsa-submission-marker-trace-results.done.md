## Story spine

Opening question: Experiment 1 found a submit-cost wall around 115--117 outstanding descriptors. Is early work already completing before that wall, or are cheap submits only buffered upstream?

Mechanism: Start tracing at a chosen submit offset; for every later submit, record submit latency and immediately poll the first four completion records. Poll step is fixed at 1.

Evidence: With offset 112 and offset 115, all first four completions are visible at the first traced poll. First success polls cost about 70--72 TSC ticks, while hot success polls settle around 22 TSC ticks. Submit #116 in the offset-115 run shows the wall-sized submit cost, but the completions were already visible at submit #115.

Implication: The admission wall is not the point where early work first becomes visible to DSA. DSA had already executed early NOOPs by the wall; the controlling effect remains admission/backpressure, with observation cost as an important measurement perturbation.
